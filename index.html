DUDE THEFT WARS
OPEN-WORLD SANDBOX

Dude Theft Wars is a stylized, physics-driven open-world action sandbox. Its official store descriptions emphasize free exploration, comedy and ragdoll physics, offline activities, missions, vehicles, and online multiplayer. It combines a playful low-poly look with a mix of relaxed exploration and more competitive sessions.

What kind of game is it?

A free-roaming sandbox where the main appeal is the combination of exploration, exaggerated physics, activities, and optional online play.

◎

Open-world freedom

Move around a colorful town, explore streets and different areas, discover points of interest, and decide which activities to try. The sandbox format is less linear than a mission-only game.

ExplorationFree roam
〰

Physics-driven comedy

Ragdoll reactions and exaggerated physics are a defining part of the game's identity. Unexpected collisions and movement can turn ordinary moments into slapstick situations.

RagdollPhysics
⌁

Mixed activities

The store descriptions mention missions and smaller activities, including driving-related jobs and casual sports mini-games. The game offers more than one way to spend time in the world.

MissionsMini-games

Distinctive art direction

The low-poly, cartoon-like look uses bold colors and simple stylized forms. The presentation is intentionally playful rather than aiming for realistic visuals.

Low-polyStylized

Solo and social play

How to enter a code

These steps describe the in-game phone interface, not external software.

  1. Load Dude Theft Wars and enter the game mode you want to play.
  2. Open the character’s in-game phone from the HUD.
  3. Open the ChEaTs.Exe app if it is available in your version.
  4. Type or paste one of the sandbox codes into the input field.
  5. Confirm using the in-game checkmark and read the game’s response. Availability can vary by update.
